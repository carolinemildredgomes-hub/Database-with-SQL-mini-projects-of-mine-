# Retail Business Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

The Retail Business Intelligence Platform simulates a real-world e-commerce analytics environment. The project helps management understand product performance, customer behavior, inventory status, and revenue generation.

## Business Questions

* Which products sell the most?
* Which customers spend the most?
* Which products require restocking?
* Which products receive the most reviews?
* Which categories generate the most revenue?

## Database Tables

* reviews
